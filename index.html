What is Ping?

Ping is an AI assistant you reach by text message. You save one phone number, text it like a contact, and it replies to that same number in seconds.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Ping runs over SMS, so there is nothing to install, no account to create, and no password to remember. You only save a number.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone that can send a text message — iPhone, Android, or an older feature phone. If it has SMS, it works.

What does it cost?

It’s free during the beta, with a fair-use cap of 100 messages a day. When the beta ends, Starter stays free and Pro is $12 a month. Anyone who joined the beta keeps free access.

Is the conversation private?

Your thread is tied to your verified number, not a shared account. There are no ads and no resale, and texting “delete” removes the conversation.
